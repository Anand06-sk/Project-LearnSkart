About EC25C07 Digital System Design

Digital system design turns logic relationships into circuits that process binary information. Students move from truth-table reasoning to organised combinational and sequential designs.

Good design practice means simplifying expressions, checking timing or state behaviour and testing the circuit against its intended specification.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why simplify a logic expression?
Simplification can reduce hardware, delay and implementation complexity.

What distinguishes sequential logic?
Its output can depend on stored state as well as current inputs.

How should I verify a design?
Compare expected and actual outputs for every relevant input or state condition.
