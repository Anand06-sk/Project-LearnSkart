About EE25C04 Basic Electronics and Electrical Engineering

This subject introduces the electrical and electronic ideas needed to read, analyse and discuss basic engineering systems. Students connect voltage, current, power and circuit elements with the behaviour of practical devices.

The course also helps learners recognise how semiconductor components and elementary electronic circuits are used in communication and control applications. Careful circuit interpretation is more useful than memorising isolated definitions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What should I learn first?
Begin with electrical quantities, circuit elements and the relationships between them.

Why are semiconductor devices included?
They are the basic building blocks of many electronic circuits used in engineering systems.

How should I practise circuits?
Draw the circuit clearly, identify known values and check units at every step.
