About CE25C02 Fluid Mechanics and Machinery

Fluid mechanics explains pressure, motion and energy in liquids and gases, while machinery applies these ideas to pumps and turbines.

Students should connect equations with flow conditions, losses, head and the practical behaviour of hydraulic systems.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is fluid mechanics important?
Water supply, drainage, irrigation and hydraulic equipment all depend on predictable flow behaviour.

How should I revise?
Practise diagrams and numerical problems alongside the assumptions behind each equation.
