About CE25404 Design of Reinforced Cement Concrete Elements

Reinforced concrete design combines concrete and steel behaviour to produce members that can carry service and factored actions safely.

Students should move from structural idealisation and loads through analysis, detailing and checks for strength and serviceability.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is detailing important?
Good detailing helps the designed member behave as intended and remain buildable.

How should I revise?
Practise the complete path from loads and assumptions to checks and reinforcement drawings.
