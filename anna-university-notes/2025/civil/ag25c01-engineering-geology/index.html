About AG25C01 Engineering Geology

Engineering geology applies knowledge of rocks, minerals, structures and geological processes to civil engineering projects.

Students learn to interpret ground conditions and recognise how natural features can affect stability, groundwater and construction risk.

What You Get on This Page

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why does geology matter?
Ground conditions strongly influence foundation behaviour, excavation and long-term performance.

How should I revise?
Relate rock and soil descriptions to the engineering problems they can create or solve.
