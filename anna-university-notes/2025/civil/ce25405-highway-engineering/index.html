About CE25405 Highway Engineering

Highway engineering combines transport planning, geometric design, pavement materials, drainage and maintenance to create safe and durable road networks.

Students should relate traffic needs, terrain, materials and construction quality to the performance of a highway.

What You Get on This Page

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What makes a highway successful?
It must serve traffic safely while remaining practical to construct, maintain and operate.

How should I revise?
Study alignment, pavement layers, drainage and maintenance as parts of one road system.
