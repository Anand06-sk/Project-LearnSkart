About IT25202 Digital Principles and System Design

Digital principles form the language of modern electronic systems. This subject helps students understand how binary values, logic gates and switching circuits combine to create the operations performed by digital hardware.

Instead of memorising complex diagrams alone, students should focus on the logic behind each circuit. Boolean expressions, simplification, timing and state transitions explain how a design works and why one arrangement is preferred over another.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why do we study Boolean algebra?
It gives a neat mathematical way to describe and simplify logic functions used in digital hardware.

What is the difference between combinational and sequential circuits?
Combinational circuits respond only to the current inputs, while sequential circuits depend on both current inputs and previous states.

How should I learn logic design?
Start with basic gates, trace truth tables, then move to simplification and practical circuit design problems.
