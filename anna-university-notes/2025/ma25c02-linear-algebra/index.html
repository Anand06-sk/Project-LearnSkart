About MA25C02 Linear Algebra

Linear algebra studies objects such as vectors and matrices and the rules used to combine them. It gives students a compact language for simultaneous equations, transformations, data representations and many computational models.

Keep the geometric and algebraic views together. A matrix is not only a table of numbers; it can describe a transformation, a system of relationships or a practical calculation pipeline.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is linear algebra useful in computing?
Vectors and matrices appear in graphics, data analysis, machine learning, networks and scientific computation.

How can I avoid matrix-calculation errors?
Write dimensions beside matrices and check that every multiplication is defined before calculating.

Should I learn geometry as well as procedures?
Yes. Geometric meaning helps you understand what an algebraic operation is doing.
