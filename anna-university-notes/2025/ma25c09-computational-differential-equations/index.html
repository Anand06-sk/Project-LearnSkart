About MA25C09 Computational Differential Equations

Computational differential equations use numerical procedures to study models when an exact symbolic solution is unavailable or inconvenient.

Students should examine step size, approximation error, stability and the connection between a numerical result and the original model.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why use numerical methods?
They provide useful approximations when direct analytical solutions are difficult.

What affects accuracy?
Step size, method choice, initial data and accumulated error all matter.
