About CS25C14 Theory of Computation

Theory of computation studies the abstract machines and formal languages used to describe computation. It gives students a precise vocabulary for discussing recognition, memory, grammar and decidability.

The subject becomes clearer when every machine is connected to the language it accepts. Build examples step by step, compare their expressive power and use traces to see where a model succeeds or reaches a boundary.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why are formal languages useful?
They provide exact rules for describing valid strings, structures and computational behaviour.

How should I study automata?
Draw the states, label transitions carefully and test the machine with accepted and rejected strings.

What does decidability mean?
It asks whether an algorithm can always determine the answer for every valid input in a problem class.
