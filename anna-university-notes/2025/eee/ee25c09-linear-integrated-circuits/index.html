About EE25C09 Linear Integrated Circuits

Linear integrated circuits combine active and passive functions in compact devices used for amplification, comparison, filtering and signal conditioning.

Students should connect ideal assumptions with practical limits such as gain, bandwidth, saturation and loading.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why use integrated circuits?
They provide compact, repeatable and practical circuit functions.

What is an ideal assumption?
It is a useful approximation that simplifies analysis while practical limits must still be checked.

How should I analyse a circuit?
Identify input, feedback, operating range and the expected signal function before calculating.
