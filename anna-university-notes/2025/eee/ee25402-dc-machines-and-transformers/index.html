About EE25402 DC Machines and Transformers

Machines and transformers convert or transfer electrical energy through magnetic coupling and electromechanical interaction.

Students should connect construction, operating principle, equivalent behaviour and performance rather than memorising names alone.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is electromagnetic energy conversion?
It is the transfer between electrical and mechanical energy through magnetic fields.

Why use a transformer?
It transfers AC energy between circuits while changing voltage and current levels.

How should I study machines?
Trace construction, field interaction, electrical output and performance measures together.
