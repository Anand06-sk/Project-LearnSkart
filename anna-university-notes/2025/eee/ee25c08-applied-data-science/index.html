About EE25C08 Applied Data Science

Applied data science uses structured data handling, visual inspection and analysis to turn observations into useful engineering evidence.

Students should ask how data was collected, what it represents and whether a conclusion is supported by an appropriate analysis.

What You Get on This Page

  • Original explanations of data preparation and engineering analysis.
  • Guidance for interpreting patterns without overstating conclusions.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why clean data?
Inconsistent or missing values can distort the result of an analysis.

What makes a conclusion useful?
It should match the data, method, assumptions and question being studied.

How should I start?
Inspect the source, structure, units and quality before applying an analysis method.
