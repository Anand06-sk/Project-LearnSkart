About ME25C03 Introduction to Mechanical Engineering

This foundation subject connects engineering materials, machines, energy conversion and manufacturing processes with the systems students encounter in practice.

Learning the purpose and operation of each system helps students build a useful mechanical-engineering vocabulary before studying advanced subjects.
