About EC25C17 Embedded Systems

Embedded systems combine hardware and software to perform dedicated functions that respond reliably to real-world conditions.

Students learn how small computing units interface with sensors, actuators and control logic in intelligent mechanical and industrial applications.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why is it relevant to mechanical engineering?
Modern equipment often uses embedded control for automation, monitoring and safety.

How should I study it?
Connect architecture to real signals, timing constraints and control logic rather than memorising blocks alone.

What should I focus on?
Interfaces, interrupts, response timing and the trade-off between hardware and software control.
