About ME25C02 Engineering Mechanics

Engineering mechanics provides a disciplined way to describe forces, moments, equilibrium and motion.

Clear free-body diagrams, consistent directions and unit checks are central to solving mechanics problems reliably.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Why draw a free-body diagram?
It separates the body from its surroundings and makes applied forces explicit.

How can I avoid mistakes?
Use one sign convention consistently and verify dimensions.
