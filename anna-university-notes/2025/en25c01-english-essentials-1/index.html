About EN25C01 English Essentials - I

Engineering work depends on communication as much as calculation. A good technical message helps a reader understand what happened, what matters and what should happen next.

Improvement comes from repeated editing: define the purpose, organise the ideas, remove vague language and check whether the reader can follow the result without guessing.

What You Get on This Page

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Is this course only about grammar?
No. Grammar supports the larger goal of communicating accurately in academic and technical situations.

How can I improve quickly?
Read a short technical passage daily and rewrite one idea in your own words while preserving its meaning.

Should technical writing use difficult words?
Prefer precise, familiar words. Clarity is more useful than unnecessary complexity.
