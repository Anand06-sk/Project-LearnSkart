Important Topics

EE3301 – Electromagnetic Fields

UNIT 1 – Electrostatics Basics

  • Gauss Law & Applications (numerical + conceptual)
  • Divergence of electric field (problems)
  • Coordinate system conversions (Cartesian, Cylindrical, Spherical)
  • Electric field intensity due to uniformly charged bodies

UNIT 2 – Electrostatics & Potential

  • Capacitance of parallel plate capacitor (with/without dielectric)
  • Polarization in dielectrics
  • Poisson's and Laplace's equations (applications)
  • Boundary conditions for electric field

UNIT 3 – Magnetostatics

  • Boundary conditions for magnetic fields
  • Magnetic field intensity (H) for:
  • Finite & infinite conductors
  • Circular coil, rectangular coil, solenoid
  • Lorentz force (applications)
  • Biot–Savart Law (numericals)
  • Ampere's Circuit Law (problems + concepts)

UNIT 4 – Time Varying Fields

  • Maxwell's equations (very important)
  • Displacement current (Id) and conduction current (Ic)
  • Relation between field theory and circuit concepts

UNIT 5 – Electromagnetic Waves

  • Poynting theorem (power flow)
  • Electromagnetic wave equation
  • Potential calculation problems (mixed numericals)
