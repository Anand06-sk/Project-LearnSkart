Important Topics

EE3701 High Voltage Engineering – Important Topics
Unit 1
  • Types of overvoltages – lightning, switching, temporary
  • Causes & effects of overvoltages on power systems
  • Travelling waves – reflection & refraction
  • Protection methods against overvoltages
  • Insulation coordination (basic concept & importance)
Unit 2
  • Properties of dielectric materials
  • Breakdown in gases
  • Uniform field
  • Non-uniform field
  • Corona discharge
  • Vacuum breakdown mechanism
  • Breakdown in liquid dielectrics (pure vs commercial oils)
  • Maintenance of insulating oil quality
  • Breakdown in solid dielectrics
  • Applications of insulating materials in equipment
Unit 3
  • Generation of high voltages
  • High DC voltage
  • High AC voltage
  • Impulse voltage generation
  • Impulse generator circuits & analysis
  • Tripping and control of impulse generators
  • Measurement of high voltages
  • Resistance & capacitance dividers
  • Peak voltmeter
  • Electrostatic & generating voltmeters
  • Sphere gap method
  • Measurement of high currents
  • Current shunts
  • Digital measurement techniques
Unit 4
  • High voltage testing of equipment
  • Insulators
  • Circuit breakers
  • Transformers
  • Bushings & isolators
  • Types of tests
  • Power frequency test
  • Impulse voltage test
  • DC testing
  • Standards (Indian & International)
  • Insulation coordination (advanced concept)
Unit 5
  • Electrostatic applications
  • Precipitation
  • Separation
  • Painting & coating
  • Printing & imaging
  • Industrial applications
  • Sand paper manufacturing
  • Smoke particle detector
  • Advanced applications
  • Electrostatic spinning
  • Pumping & propulsion
  • Ozone generation
  • Biomedical applications
