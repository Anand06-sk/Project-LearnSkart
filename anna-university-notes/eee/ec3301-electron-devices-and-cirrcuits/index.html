Important Topics

EC3301 – Electron Devices and Circuits

UNIT 1 – Diodes and Rectifiers

  • PN junction diode (forward & reverse bias, V-I characteristics)
  • Diode equation & resistance
  • Half wave rectifier (working, waveform, efficiency, ripple factor)
  • Full wave rectifier (center tap & bridge, comparison)
  • Rectifier parameters (PIV, ripple factor, efficiency)
  • Zener diode (breakdown, characteristics)
  • Zener as voltage regulator

UNIT 2 & 3 – Bipolar and Field Effect Transistors

  • BJT (NPN, PNP) characteristics
  • Regions of operation (cut-off, active, saturation)
  • CE, CB, CC configurations (comparison very important)
  • Current gain (α, β relations)
  • Load line analysis
  • MOSFET (enhancement & depletion, characteristics)
  • MOSFET vs BJT
  • SCR (working, triggering methods)
  • UJT (relaxation oscillator concept)

UNIT 4 – Amplifier Characteristics

  • CMRR (definition, formula, importance)
  • Single tuned amplifier (working, frequency response)
  • Neutralization (concept & need)

UNIT 5 – Feedback and Oscillators

  • Positive & negative feedback
  • Voltage series feedback amplifier
  • Barkhausen criterion
  • Wein bridge oscillator
  • Hartley oscillator
  • RC phase shift oscillator
