BE3252 – Basic Electrical, Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering Notes – Regulation 2021

BE3252 Basic Electrical, Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering provides civil engineers with fundamental knowledge of electrical systems, power distribution, electronic circuits, and sensor technologies used in civil projects. Important Topics

BE3252 Basic Electrical, Electronics and Instrumentation Engineering, unit-wise:

Unit 1
  • Kirchhoff's laws (KVL & KCL) with circuit analysis
  • Basic circuit elements (resistor, inductor, capacitor) and their roles
  • Star and delta connections in three-phase systems
  • Series and parallel equivalent circuits (R, L, C)

Unit 2
  • Magnetic concepts (MMF, flux density, mutual inductance)
  • Earthing (importance, methods, functions)
  • Circuit breakers (types, classification, working)
  • Domestic wiring system and accessories
  • Basic wiring layout and safety concepts

Unit 3
  • DC generator (construction & working)
  • DC motor (construction & principle)
  • Single-phase transformer (working principle)
  • Three-phase alternator vs induction motor
  • Induction motor vs synchronous motor

Unit 4
  • PN junction diode characteristics
  • Zener diode and voltage regulation
  • BJT configurations and biasing
  • Rectifier circuits (half-wave & full-wave)
  • SCR characteristics and applications
  • MOSFET (basic working)

Unit 5
  • Sensors and transducers (types & industrial applications)
  • LVDT (working and output cases)
  • Optical encoder (working principle)
  • Hall effect and strain gauge
  • Actuators and limit switches
  • Proximity and photo sensors
