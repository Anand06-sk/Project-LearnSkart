PH3201 – Physics For Civil Engineering Notes – Regulation 2021

PH3201 Physics for Civil Engineering applies physics principles to civil engineering problems, focusing on thermal comfort, acoustics, and materials science relevant to building design and construction. Use the listed materials with the prescribed syllabus to plan revision.

Important Topics

PH3201 Physics for Civil Engineering, unit-wise:

Unit 1
  • Thermal performance of buildings (factors & heat gain/loss)
  • Modes of heat transfer (conduction, convection, radiation)
  • Thermal insulation in buildings
  • Solar radiation and climate-based building design
  • Shading devices (types & applications)
  • Thermal conductivity measurement (basic idea)

Unit 2
  • Air conditioning systems (types & applications)
  • Window air conditioner (construction & working)
  • Natural ventilation (types, advantages, disadvantages)
  • Wind-driven and stack ventilation
  • Fan coil system and chilled water plant
  • Fire safety in AC systems (precautions)

Unit 3
  • Sound measurement and absorption coefficient
  • Noise control methods in buildings
  • Sound absorbing materials
  • Principles of artificial lighting
  • Glare and its reduction
  • Daylight factor and building lighting design

Unit 4
  • Manufacturing methods of ceramics (basic idea)
  • Properties and applications of ceramics
  • Shape memory alloys (properties & uses)
  • Composites in construction (FRP, FRM)
  • Ferroelectric and ferromagnetic materials

Unit 5
  • Earthquake concepts (types, intensity, magnitude)
  • Seismograph (working principle)
  • Seismic hazard analysis (basic idea)
  • Fire hazards (types, causes, prevention)
  • Fireproofing materials
  • Cyclone formation (basic concept)
