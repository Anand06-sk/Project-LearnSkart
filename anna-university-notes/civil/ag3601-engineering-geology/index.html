AG3601 – Engineering Geology Notes – Regulation 2021

AG3601 Engineering Geology covers geological principles essential for civil engineering projects, including rock and mineral identification, groundwater analysis, and site investigation techniques. Use the listed materials with the prescribed syllabus to plan revision.

Important Topics

AG3601 Engineering Geology, unit-wise:

Topics Covered:
  • Internal structure of earth (Layers, composition)
  • Groundwater (Movement, role in civil engineering, effects on structures)
  • Minerals (Physical properties with examples)
  • Feldspar group (Composition, properties, uses)
  • Folds (Parts, types with sketches)
  • Geological Strength Index (GSI) (Concept & importance)
  • Ground Penetrating Radar (GPR) (Working principle, applications)
  • Electrical resistivity method (Geophysical investigation, subsurface identification)
  • Landslides (Causes, classification, prevention methods)
  • Geological investigation for tunnels (Site investigation, challenges, precautions)
