GE3152 – Heritage of Tamils Notes – Regulation 2021

GE3152 is a core subject for Anna University Semester 1 students, introducing the rich heritage, literature, and cultural contributions of Tamils. Use the listed materials with the prescribed syllabus to plan revision.

Important Topics

UNIT I – LANGUAGE AND LITERATURE

PART-A (2 MARKS)
  • Language families in India
  • Dravidian languages – features
  • Tamil as a classical language (criteria)
  • Sangam literature – basic features
  • Secular nature of Sangam literature
  • Thirukkural – author and structure
  • Aham vs Puram concept
  • Tamil epics – names and authors
  • Bhakti literature – Azhwars & Nayanmars
  • Bharathiyar & Bharathidasan contributions
PART-B (13 MARKS)
  • Explain characteristics of Dravidian languages and Tamil as a classical language
  • Discuss Sangam literature and its secular nature
  • Explain distributive justice in Sangam literature
  • Management principles in Thirukkural
  • Tamil epics and influence of Buddhism & Jainism
  • Bhakti movement in Tamil Nadu (Azhwars & Nayanmars)
  • Development of modern Tamil literature
  • Contributions of Bharathiyar and Bharathidasan

UNIT II – HERITAGE AND ART FORMS

PART-A (2 MARKS)
  • Rock art paintings
  • Hero stones (Nadukal)
  • Bronze icons
  • Terracotta sculptures
  • Temple car (Ther) making
  • Village deities
  • Thiruvalluvar statue (Kanyakumari)
  • Tribal handicrafts
  • Musical instruments (Parai, Veena, Yazh, Nadaswaram, Mridangam)
PART-B (13 MARKS)
  • Evolution from rock art to modern art
  • Hero stones to modern sculpture – development
  • Bronze sculpture tradition in Tamil Nadu
  • Terracotta art and its significance
  • Temple car making – process and cultural role
  • Tribal arts and handicrafts
  • Musical instruments of Tamils – making and importance
  • Role of temples in social and economic life

UNIT III – FOLK AND MARTIAL ARTS

PART-A (2 MARKS)
  • Therukoothu
  • Karagattam
  • Villu Pattu
  • Kaniyan Koothu
  • Oyilattam
  • Leather puppetry
  • Silambattam
  • Valari
  • Tiger dance
PART-B (13 MARKS)
  • Explain major Tamil folk arts with examples
  • Therukoothu and Villu Pattu – structure and significance
  • Traditional dance forms and their cultural role
  • Martial arts of Tamils – Silambam & Valari
  • Sports and games of Tamils – cultural importance

UNIT IV – THINAI AND SANGAM LIFE

PART-A (2 MARKS)
  • Thinai classification
  • Aham and Puram
  • Flora and fauna in Sangam age
  • Aram concept
  • Education in Sangam period
  • Sangam cities and ports
  • Trade (export/import)
  • Chola overseas conquest
PART-B (13 MARKS)
  • Explain Thinai concept with classification
  • Aham vs Puram in detail
  • Aram (ethics) in Tamil culture
  • Education and literacy in Sangam age
  • Ancient cities and ports
  • Trade and commerce during Sangam period
  • Overseas expeditions of Cholas

UNIT V – CONTRIBUTION OF TAMILS

PART-A (2 MARKS)
  • Tamil role in freedom struggle
  • Self-Respect Movement
  • Siddha medicine
  • Tamil inscriptions & manuscripts
  • Print history of Tamil books
  • Cultural influence of Tamils
PART-B (13 MARKS)
  • Contribution of Tamils to Indian freedom movement
  • Cultural influence of Tamils across India
  • Self-Respect Movement – impact and ideology
  • Siddha medicine system – principles and importance
  • Inscriptions and manuscripts – historical value
  • Evolution of Tamil printing and publishing
