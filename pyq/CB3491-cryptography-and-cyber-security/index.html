Unit-wise Important Questions

Important CB3491 questions from each unit.

Unit I Introduction and Number Theory

Part A (2 Marks)

  • Define Cryptography and Cryptanalysis.
  • What is the difference between a Monoalphabetic and Polyalphabetic cipher?
  • Define Steganography and its common techniques.
  • Explain the Avalanche Effect in encryption.
Part B (13/16 Marks)
  • OSI Security Architecture: Explain the model with a diagram.
  • Classical Encryption Techniques: Discuss substitution and transposition techniques with examples.
  • Model for Network Security: Explain its components and working mechanism.
  • Foundations of Modern Cryptography: Explain perfect security and information theory.

Unit II Symmetric Ciphers

Part A (2 Marks)

  • State the disadvantages of Double DES.
  • What are the four stages of the AES encryption process?
  • Explain the role of S-boxes in the DES algorithm.
  • Briefly describe the working principle of the RC4 stream cipher.
Part B (13/16 Marks)
  • Data Encryption Standard (DES): Explain its architecture, encryption process, and strength.
  • Advanced Encryption Standard (AES): Explain the round functions and compare AES with DES.
  • Block Cipher Modes of Operation: Explain ECB, CBC, CFB, OFB, and Counter modes.
  • Mathematical Foundations: Explain groups, rings, and finite fields in symmetric key cryptography.

Unit III Asymmetric Cryptography

Part A (2 Marks)

  • State Euler's Totient function and its significance.
  • What is the Discrete Logarithm Problem?
  • Define Public-Key Certificate and its requirements.
Part B (13/16 Marks)
  • RSA Algorithm: Explain key generation, encryption/decryption, and security threats.
  • Diffie-Hellman Key Exchange: Explain the mechanism with an example and its man-in-the-middle vulnerability.
  • Chinese Remainder Theorem (CRT): State and prove the theorem and describe its cryptographic applications.
  • Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC): Explain arithmetic operations, ECC Diffie-Hellman, and its advantages over RSA.

Unit IV Message Authentication and Integrity

Part A (2 Marks)

  • Distinguish between a Hash function and a Message Authentication Code (MAC).
  • What are the requirements for a digital signature?
  • Briefly explain the role of X.509 certificates in security.
Part B (13/16 Marks)
  • Hash Functions: Explain SHA and the structure of HMAC.
  • Digital Signatures: Explain the principles, requirements, and standard algorithms.
  • Authentication Protocols: Explain Kerberos architecture and certification mechanisms.

Unit V Cyber Security and Social Engineering

Part A (2 Marks)

  • What is SQL injection and what are its potential consequences?
  • Define phishing and its impact on cyber security.
  • What is the difference between active and passive security threats?
Part B (13/16 Marks)
  • Classification of Cyber Crimes: Provide an overview with examples.
  • Network Security: Discuss cloud, web, and wireless security protocols.
  • Tools and Methods: Describe common cybercrime techniques and their countermeasures.

Most Repeated / High-Weight Questions

DES and AES, RSA key generation, Diffie-Hellman key exchange, hash functions, digital signatures, block cipher modes, and cybercrime classification and countermeasures.
